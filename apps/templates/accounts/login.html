About the ARSP Logo
The ARSP logo represents the intersection of scientific expertise, technological innovation, and collaborative action in the fight against antimicrobial resistance. Each element - the microscope for research, the computer for surveillance, and the pills and flasks for intervention - symbolizes a core objective of the program. Together, they reflect ARSP's commitment to protecting public health through a multi-faceted approach to monitoring, understanding, and addressing the spread of resistant pathogens.
